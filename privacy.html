3. Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly (e.g., authentication, theme preference).

  • Advertising cookies: Used to display relevant ads and measure ad performance.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.
